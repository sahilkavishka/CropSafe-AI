STATEMENT OF OFFENSES & CHARGES

COUNT 1: Sale, storage, and distribution of adulterated fertilizer contrary to Section 8(1) of the Regulation of Fertilizers Act No. 68 of 1988.

That the said Accused on or about 24-09-2026 at Economic Center Wholesale Complex, Block D, Dambulla, within the jurisdiction of this Court, did possess, distribute, and offer for commercial sale 120.0 Metric Tons of fertilizer bearing Batch No: BATCH-LK-2026-X89 labeled as 'Urea (Granular)', which had been materially adulterated with Crushed Gypsum & Recycled Industrial Slag (18.5% by weight), thereby failing to comply with the compulsory quality standards prescribed under SLS 618:2014, an offense punishable under Section 19(1) of Act No. 68 of 1988.

COUNT 2: Failure to maintain statutory specifications and distribution of prohibited substances contrary to Section 19(2) of Act No. 68 of 1988.

That the Accused at the same time and place aforesaid did introduce non-nutritive foreign matter into commercial fertilizer streams causing hazard to agriculture and food safety, thereby rendering the seized consignment of 120.0 MT liable to complete forfeiture and destruction.
